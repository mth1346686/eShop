# ⚜️ Atelier Shop — Luxury Boutique Cairo

An elite, high-performance luxury e-commerce boutique platform handcrafted for discerning clients in Cairo and across Egypt. Atelier Shop showcases master-crafted chronographs, Tuscan full-grain leather, rare botanical oud extraits, and titanium artisan accessories.

---

## 💎 Features & Architecture

### 1. Slide-Over Shopping Bag Drawer
- Smooth hardware-accelerated drawer sliding animation.
- Instant item count and subtotal reactivity.
- Dynamic quantity management (`+`, `-`, and remove items).
- Persistent state saved automatically via `localStorage`.

### 2. VIP Free Express Delivery Progress Bar
- Real-time progress bar tracking cart value towards free express shipping.
- Calibrated to trigger free delivery across Egypt when the cart total exceeds **150 EGP**.

### 3. Exclusively Cash on Delivery (COD) Checkout
- Tailored for the Egyptian luxury market: **الدفع عند الاستلام والمعاينة** (Pay upon delivery & inspection).
- Instant WhatsApp VIP Concierge order dispatch with full order breakdown, items list, customer address, and unique order tracking ID.

### 4. Secret Coupon Engine
- Secret promo codes engine strictly kept confidential and not advertised on the public UI.
- Supports tiered percentage discounts (`VIP50`, `LUXE20`, `ATELIER15`, `ATELIER10`).
- Validated client-side with instant subtotal and total recalculations.

### 5. Tactile Web Audio Synthesizer
- Built-in Web Audio API frequency synthesis offering subtle, pleasant haptic-like audible feedback on cart interactions and button clicks.
- Toggleable sound icon (`🔊 / 🔇`) to respect user preference.

### 6. Dual Luxury Theme Engine
- **Dark Luxury**: Deep obsidian (`#0d0d12`), charcoal cards (`#101015`), and glowing imperial gold accents (`#f59e0b`).
- **Light Luxury**: Warm alabaster (`#f8f7f2`), crisp white cards (`#ffffff`), and polished champagne gold details.

### 7. Saved Wishlist Drawer & Quick View Modal
- Wishlist drawer with instant "Move All to Bag" capability.
- Quick View modal (`👁️`) for inspecting high-resolution photos, materials, and artisan craft details before buying.
